# SchemeForge 40k faction roadmap

The goal is one representative original model-map archetype and at least one Squidmar/Vallejo-based colour scheme for every primary Warhammer 40,000 army. The scope follows the broad army identities presented by Games Workshop rather than treating every Space Marine chapter or Army of Renown as a separate faction.

Status reviewed on 2026-09-15 against the official Warhammer 40,000 army overview: <https://warhammer40000.com/the-armies/>

## Delivery rule

Work proceeds in waves of roughly five factions. Every checked entry must include:

1. An original WebP illustration following `model-art-direction.md`.
2. A `ModelDefinition` with archetype-specific percentage markers.
3. A ready-to-add scheme using only the owned Vallejo Essential, Dark Future and Fantasy sets.
4. A successful production build and CI run.
